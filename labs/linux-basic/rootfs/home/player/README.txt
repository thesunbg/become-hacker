If you are reading this, the laptop made it to you.

I am not going to tell you who I am. You would not believe me, and it would not help
you anyway. What I will tell you is this:

  If you want to understand the Internet, stop using it like a normal user.

A normal user clicks what they are shown and sees what they are given. You have been
doing that your whole life, and it is why the machine in front of you feels like magic.
It is not magic. It is a filesystem, some processes, and a pile of conventions that
everybody agreed to and then quietly forgot to question.

So: start by finding out where you are.

Who are you on this machine? Which directory are you standing in? These sound like
trivial questions. They are not. They are the only two questions that matter, because
everything you are allowed to do here follows from the answer.

I left something else for you as well. A normal user would never see it.
